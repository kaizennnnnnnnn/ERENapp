## 1. What Eren is

Eren is a small app for two people to look after one virtual cat together. Two
people share a **household**: one cat, one shared history of messages, notes,
photos, moods and games.

Eren is a toy. It is not a health, safety, medical, veterinary, financial or
emergency service. Nothing in it is advice. The cat is not real and the app does
not monitor anything in the physical world.

## 3. Households and invite codes

A household holds two people. You join one by entering another person's invite
code.

**Treat your invite code like a password.** Anyone who has it and creates an
account can join your household and will then be able to read your shared
messages, notes, photos and moods. Only send it to the person you mean to share
with.

If a code gets out, replace it: **Profile → NEW CODE**. The old code stops working
immediately.

You can leave a household at any time: **Profile → LEAVE THIS HOME**. The shared
history stays with the person who remains, and the invite code is replaced behind
you, so the code you knew will not let you back in. If you are the only person in
the household, leaving deletes the household and everything in it.

Do not use Eren to store or move material you would not want a human being to see

## 5. Reporting and blocking

Every message, note and memory in Eren has a **report** action. Use it if you see
something that breaks §4.

You can also **report the person** you share a household with, and **block** them.
Blocking ends the shared household immediately and permanently stops that account
from joining a household with yours again — including if they somehow obtain a
future invite code.

Reports come to us with the specific item and enough context to act on it. We
review them and, depending on what we find, we may do nothing, remove content,
warn an account, suspend it, close it, or report it to law enforcement.

If you report something, we may need to look at the reported content in order to
act. That is one of the narrow circumstances the [Privacy Policy](/privacy)
describes.

**If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.** We
are not an emergency service and we do not monitor Eren in real time.

## 7. The AI cat

The "Talk to Eren" screen is a language model — software — pretending to be a cat.
It is not a person, not a professional, and not aware of anything outside your
conversation.

- What it says is generated, and can be wrong.
- Do not rely on it for anything that matters.
- Your conversation with it is private to your own account: the person you share a
  household with cannot read it.
- What you type there is sent to **Anthropic** to produce a reply. See the
  [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

There is a limit on how many messages you can send per day. It exists to keep the
app affordable to run, and may change.
