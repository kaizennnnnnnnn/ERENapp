## 1. The short version

- You sign in with an email address and a password, and pick a display name.
- Two people join a **household** and share one cat. Your partner can see the
  things you do in the shared parts of the app.
- Messages you write to your partner, notes you pin, and photos you upload are
  stored on our servers. They are not end-to-end encrypted. We can technically
  access them; we do not read them except where §7 says otherwise.
- The "Talk to Eren" screen sends your message and some context to **Anthropic**,
  who run the AI model. Anthropic processes it for us and deletes it within 30 days.
- Push notifications are optional and only work if you allow them.
- You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, or by
  emailing us.
- Eren is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it.

## 2. What we collect, and why

### 2.1 Your account
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | To create your account, sign you in, and send password resets. |
| Password | Stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider. We never see or store your actual password. |
| Display name | Shown to your partner and used by Eren when he talks to you. |
| A random account ID (UUID) | To link your data together internally. |

### 2.2 Your household
When you create or join a household you and one other person are linked
together. We store the household name, an invite code, your household's time
zone, and optionally the cat's "birthday" and your anniversary date.

**Being in a household means your partner can see:** your display name, your
daily mood entries and any note attached to them, the care actions you take,
the messages and notes you send them, the photos and memories you add, your
game scores, your birthday if you set one, and how long you have spent in the
app. This is the entire point of the app, but you should know it before you
type anything.

Your "Talk to Eren" conversations are the exception — see §2.6.

### 2.3 Things you write
- **Messages to your partner** ("couple journal") — free text, stored until
  deleted.
- **Notes on the note board** — free text and small in-game gifts.
- **Memories** — free text, tags, and optional photos.
- **Daily mood** — one of five moods per day, plus an optional free-text note.
- **Reminders** — a title and description you write.
- **Names you choose** — household name, and similar.

We store all of this so the app works: so your partner receives it, and so it
is still there tomorrow.

### 2.4 Photos you upload
If you add a photo to a memory, the image file is uploaded to our storage
provider and kept until you or your partner delete that memory or you delete
your account. We do not scan, analyse or run face recognition on your photos,
and they are never sent to the AI model.

Eren does not ask Android for permission to read your photo library. When you
add a photo, your device's own file picker opens and only the single file you
choose is sent.

### 2.5 What you do in the app
We record care actions (feeding, washing, playing and so on) with a timestamp
and who did them, the length of your app sessions, mini-game scores and weekly
results, and in-game progress such as items, coins, levels and streaks. This is
what makes the cat's state, the shared history and the leaderboards work, and
it is shown back to you and your partner inside the app.

### 2.6 Talking to Eren (the AI feature)
The "Talk to Eren" screen is a conversation with a large language model. **The
replies are generated by AI. Eren is not a person.**

When you send a message, the following is transmitted to **Anthropic** to
generate the reply:

- the message you just typed;
- your recent conversation history on that screen;
- your first name and your partner's first name;
- the cat's current stats and mood, your level, the local time of day;
- a short list of recent care actions and who did them;
- any facts Eren has previously saved about you (see below).

Your partner's messages to you, your photos, your mood entries, and your
partner's own conversations with Eren are **not** sent.

Eren can save short durable facts about you as you talk — things like your
routines, the people you mention, or something you said you were worried about.
These are stored against your account, capped at 60, and re-sent with each
message so he remembers. You can see them in the app. Anything you tell Eren
may end up saved this way, so do not tell him anything you would not want
written down.

**Your conversations with Eren are private to you.** They are enforced at the
database level as readable only by your own account; your partner cannot open
them.

### 2.7 Notifications
If you allow notifications, your browser or device creates a push subscription
— a unique address for your device plus two encryption keys — and we store it
so we can send you a notification when the app is closed. You can revoke this
at any time in your device or browser settings, and in the app's own
notification settings.

Notification text can include a short preview (up to 140 characters) of a
message your partner sent you, so it will appear on your lock screen. The
notification content is encrypted so that the push service delivering it cannot
read it (Web Push encryption, RFC 8291).

### 2.8 What we do NOT collect
No location data. No contacts or address book. No calendar. No microphone or
camera access. No advertising identifier. No web browsing history. No health or
fitness data. No payment or financial data — Eren is free and has nothing to
buy. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party advertising or
measurement SDKs of any kind.

### 2.9 Technical logs
Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs which include IP
addresses, timestamps and request paths, and our authentication provider
records sign-in events with the IP address they came from. These exist to keep
the service running and to detect abuse. We do not use them to build a profile
of you. Retention: [PLACEHOLDER: state your providers' log retention — typically

## 3. Legal basis (for users in the EU/EEA and the UK)

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR we rely on:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating and running your account; the household; the cat; messages, notes, memories and photos; the AI chat; game state | **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) — this is the service you signed up for. |
| Push notifications | **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — given by allowing the browser/OS notification permission and the in-app toggles, withdrawable at any time without affecting anything else. |
| Optional details: your birthday, your anniversary, your mood note | **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — you choose whether to fill these in; leave them blank and the app still works. |
| Server logs, rate limiting, abuse prevention, keeping the service secure | **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) — our interest in a service that stays up and is not abused, balanced against the very limited data involved. |

Withdrawing consent for the optional items is done by turning the toggle off or
clearing the field; it does not affect the lawfulness of anything done before.

We do not ask you for special category data (Art. 9) and Eren has no feature
that requires it. Please do not put health information, or anything else
sensitive, into free-text fields.

## 8. Security

- All traffic between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS. Data is
  encrypted at rest on our providers' infrastructure.
- Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider. We never receive them.
- Every table in our database has **row-level security** enabled: the database
  itself, not just the app code, enforces that you can only read your own data
  and your own household's data.
- Your "Talk to Eren" conversation is restricted at the database level to your
  own account, so your partner cannot read it even though you share a household.
- Photos you upload are held in **private** storage. They have no public web
  address. When you open your memory wall the app requests a temporary link
  that expires within the hour, and one is only issued if you are a member of
  the household the photo belongs to.
- Push notification content is encrypted end-to-end between our server and your
  device, so the push delivery service cannot read it.
- Our administrative database key and AI provider key are held only on the
  server and are never sent to your device. Scheduled background jobs are
  authenticated with a shared secret.
- Your household invite code lets someone join your household. **Treat it like
  a password** — anyone who has it and creates an account can join your
  household and will then be able to see your shared messages, notes, photos
  and moods. Only send it to your partner.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal
data we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where
the law requires it, and will tell you directly if the breach is likely to put
you at high risk.
